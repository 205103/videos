# Notes for Claude

- Skills are in `.claude/skills/`, subagents in `.claude/agents/`.
- The Digital Samba video toolkit is bundled at `vendor/claude-code-video-toolkit` and `$VIDEO_TOOLKIT_ROOT` points there.
